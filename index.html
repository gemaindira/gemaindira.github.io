My story

Curiosity has taken me places

I'm a curious person, and that curiosity has taken me across three continents. Every move taught me to adapt, to learn fast, and to feel at home in something new.

My career has been one of exploration too. I started in marketing in Ecuador, studied in Beijing, and came to Lund to learn about entrepreneurship. There I realised that what really drives me is finding solutions and creating value, and that led me to product management. I'm still exploring, and I keep myself open to new challenges.

What I work on
